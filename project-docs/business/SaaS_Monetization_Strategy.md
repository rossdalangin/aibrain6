# Monetization & SaaS Strategy - Nexus AI Workforce

## Pricing Model (Targeted at Businesses & Agencies)

### 1. Free (Community Version)
- **Features:** 1 AI Employee, Basic Chat, No Knowledge Base, SQLite storage.
- **Target:** Solopreneurs and testing.

### 2. Pro ($197/mo)
- **Features:** 10 AI Employees, Departments, Basic RAG (PDF/URLs), Workflow Templates, Analytics.
- **Target:** Small to mid-sized businesses.

### 3. Agency ($497/mo)
- **Features:** Unlimited AI Employees, White Labeling (Client Portals), Multi-site support, priority API support, Marketplace access.
- **Target:** Marketing and AI agencies.

### 4. Enterprise (Dynamic, defaults to $997/mo)
- **Management:** Configured dynamically under Settings (`nexus_plan_enterprise_price`). Supports custom invoicing adjustments.
- **Fulfillment:** Cryptographically signed license key unlock. Activates unlimited AI Employees, dedicated SSO, custom Pinecone vector DB integration, and full audit trailing.
- **Features:** Custom AI Models (Fine-tuned), dedicated vector DB (Pinecone/Weaviate), Single Sign-On (SSO), Audit Logs, 24/7 Support.
- **Target:** Large corporations and regulated industries.

## Monetization Strategy
- **Direct Sales:** Monthly/Annual subscriptions for the plugin license.
- **Token Markup (Optional):** Agencies can resell AI usage to clients with a margin via an integrated credit system.
- **Marketplace:** A platform where developers and prompt engineers can sell specialized AI Employee "Personas" and "Workflow Blueprints," with Nexus taking a 20-30% cut.
- **Implementation Services:** Offering white-glove setup and training for enterprise clients.

## White-Label Strategy (Agency Mode)
- **Branding:** Allow agencies to replace "Nexus AI" with their own logo, colors, and domain.
- **Client Management:** A centralized dashboard for agencies to manage AI workforces across multiple client WordPress sites.
- **Custom UI:** Custom login pages and restricted dashboards for the agency's clients.

## Marketing Strategy
- **Content Marketing:** Case studies showing how companies replaced manual roles with AI Workforce agents.
- **Partnerships:** Collaborating with WordPress hosting providers and popular plugin developers (e.g., WooCommerce).
- **Influencer Outreach:** Engaging with AI and productivity influencers on LinkedIn and YouTube.
- **The "AI Efficiency" Audit:** A free tool that analyzes a business's workflows and identifies which roles can be augmented by Nexus AI.
