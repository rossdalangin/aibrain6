# NEXUS AI: STRATEGIC MARKETING PLAN 2025

- **High-End Agencies:** Reselling AIaaS for $1k-$5k/mo.
- **Mid-Market E-commerce:** Automated support and SEO management.
- **Knowledge-Heavy Consulting:** Scaling expertise through RAG-powered agents.

## 3. Core Messaging Angles
- "Your $5,000,000 Executive Team for $497/mo."
- "The only AI that actually manages your WordPress site."
- "Stop prompts. Start outcomes."

## 4. Scaling Strategy
- Launch an Affiliate Program for WP agencies.
- Host "AI Efficiency" webinars twice a month.
- Build the "Nexus Certified Partner" ecosystem.
