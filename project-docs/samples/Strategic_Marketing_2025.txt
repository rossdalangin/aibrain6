# STRATEGIC MARKETING PLAN 2025: NEXUS AI EXPANSION

## Executive Summary
This document outlines the high-level strategy for scaling Nexus AI Workforce as the dominant AI Operating System for the WordPress ecosystem.

## 1. Brand Narrative
Nexus AI is not a chatbot; it is a workforce multiplier. We position the platform as "The Virtual HQ for the Modern Enterprise."

## 2. Target Segments
- **WordPress Agencies:** High-volume white-labeling for client portfolios.
- **E-commerce (WooCommerce):** Automation of product descriptions, customer support, and sales conversion loops.
- **SaaS Founders:** Building virtual departments for development and QA.

## 3. Revenue Architecture
- **Recurring Tiered Pricing:** $197/mo (Pro) to $497/mo (Agency).
- **Consumable Credits:** Pay-as-you-go token model for high-volume users.
- **Marketplace Royalties:** 30% commission on third-party agent installs.

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Workforce Retention:** Percentage of users who keep agents active for > 3 months.
- **Brain Depth:** Average document count per Company Brain.
- **Consensus Rate:** Number of multi-agent meetings successfully finalized.

## 5. Deployment Roadmap
- **Q1:** Launch AI Marketplace.
- **Q2:** Real-time Voice Integration for agents.
- **Q3:** On-premise Ollama fleet management for Enterprise.
