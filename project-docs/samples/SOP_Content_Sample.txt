# NEXUS AI: SOP - CONTENT PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

## 1. Research Phase
- **Agent:** Research Specialist (Temperature 0.3)
- **Task:** Analyze the top 10 search results for the target keyword and extract key data points, common questions, and missing information.

## 2. Drafting Phase
- **Agent:** Creative Copywriter (Temperature 0.9)
- **Task:** Create a 1,500-word long-form article using the research data. Tone must be engaging and align with the 'Future-Forward' brand voice.

## 3. SEO Optimization Phase
- **Agent:** SEO Expert (Temperature 0.2)
- **Task:** Audit the draft for keyword density, header structure, and internal link opportunities.

## 4. Final Review & Publish
- **Agent:** Content Manager (Temperature 0.4)
- **Task:** Perform final grammar check and format for WordPress Gutenberg blocks. Set status to 'Draft' and notify the Admin.
