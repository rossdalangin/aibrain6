================================================================================
NEXUS AI SYSTEMS: BATTLE-TESTED SEO CONTENT & KEYWORD OPTIMIZATION CHECKLIST
================================================================================

This document represents our official search engine optimization (SEO) standard.
All copywriters and SEO agents must check this checklist when drafting content
or auditing landing pages.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: KEYWORD DENSITY AND POSITIONING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Primary Keywords: Must be placed in:
  - The main H1 title.
  - The first 100 words of the introduction.
  - At least two H2 or H3 subheadings.
* Keyword Density: Keep keyword density strictly between 1.2% and 1.8%.
  Avoid keyword stuffing. If the density exceeds 2.0%, flag for re-writing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: CONTENT STRUCTURE AND READABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Paragraph Length: Keep paragraphs short. Maximum 3-4 sentences (under 60 words).
* Sentence Length: Keep sentences under 20 words for premium mobile readability.
* Bullet Points: Every article must include at least one bulleted list or a
  structured key takeaways table to increase user session duration.
* Subheading Frequency: Include a descriptive H2/H3 subheading every 250-300 words.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: METADATA AND INTERNAL LINKING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Meta Title: Must be under 60 characters and contain the primary keyword.
* Meta Description: Must be under 155 characters and contain a clear call to action.
* Internal Links: Every draft post must suggest at least two links to other related
  internal resource pages (e.g. /features or /pricing).
================================================================================
