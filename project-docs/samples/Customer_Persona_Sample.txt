# NEXUS AI: TARGET CUSTOMER PERSONA - "THE AGENCY ACCELERATOR"

## 1. Demographics
- **Role:** Founder of a WordPress/Marketing Agency.
- **Team Size:** 5-15 employees.
- **Current Problem:** High labor costs, inconsistent output quality, difficulty scaling service delivery.

## 2. Psychographics
- **Aspirations:** Wants to transition from a "labor shop" to a "high-margin technology partner."
- **Fears:** Being left behind by AI; data security for clients.
- **Values:** Efficiency, white-labeling, scalability.

## 3. The Nexus AI Fit
- Uses **White Label** mode to brand the platform as their own.
- Uses **Multi-Agent Workflows** to automate 80% of content production.
- Uses **RAG** to ensure the AI knows every client's unique business details.
