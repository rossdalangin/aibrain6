================================================================================
NEXUS AI SYSTEMS: OFFICIAL CUSTOMER REFUND & SUBSCRIPTION CANCELLATION POLICY
================================================================================

This document outlines the standard operational guidelines, rules, and SLA limits
concerning product refunds, subscription cancellations, and billing disputes.
All customer support agents must consult this brain document prior to answering
client billing questions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: THE 14-DAY RISK-FREE GUARANTEE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* All new SaaS subscriptions (including Pro, Agency, and Enterprise tiers) are
  eligible for our 14-Day Risk-Free Trial Guarantee.
* If a customer submits a refund request within 14 calendar days of their initial
  purchase, they are entitled to a full 100% refund. No questions asked.
* Refund requests submitted after Day 14 are NOT eligible for a refund.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: SUBSCRIPTION CANCELLATION RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Customers can cancel their subscription at any time directly through their
  "Plans & Billing" workspace dashboard.
* Upon cancellation, their premium access remains active until the end of their
  current billing period.
* On the billing expiration date, the plugin automatically removes the local
  cryptographic licensing signature, safely downgrading the workspace capacity
  back to the Starter/Free tier (1 active agent limit) without losing or breaking
  existing employee files or database records.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: DISPUTES AND CHARGEBACKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* If a customer opens a billing dispute or credit card chargeback without
  contacting support first, their active workspace license is flagged as
  "license_compromised_alert" in the system audit trail and is suspended.
* To avoid suspension, customers should contact our finance team directly at:
  billing@nexus-ai-saas.com.
================================================================================
