# Nexus AI Workforce: Agency Success Kit

## 1. The "AI-as-a-Service" (AIaaS) Model
Stop selling manual labor. Start selling AI outcomes. As an agency, you can build specialized AI Workforces for your clients and charge a recurring monthly management fee.

## 2. Recommended Service Packages

- **Outcome:** 8 high-quality, SEO-optimized articles per month.
- **Your Effort:** Setup the RAG knowledge base once; monitor monthly.

### The Support Optimizer ($497/mo)
- **Deployment:** Knowledge Base agent + Customer Support Assistant.
- **Outcome:** AI-drafted responses for all common support tickets.
- **Your Effort:** Periodic updates to the Company Brain.

## 3. White-Label Customization
Use the **Nexus White Label** settings to:
- Replace Nexus branding with your agency logo.
- Set primary UI colors to match your brand.
- Create "Client Portals" where clients can interact with their AI team via a premium, dedicated interface.
- Deploy the **Frontend Chat Widget** so clients can provide immediate value to their site visitors using their specialized AI workforce.

## 4. Scaling Your Revenue
Utilize the **AI Marketplace Export** feature to build "Proven AI Teams." Once you have a high-performing agent setup, export the bundle and deploy it for 50 other clients in minutes.
