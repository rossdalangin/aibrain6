# Nexus AI Workforce: Step-by-Step Master Tutorial

This tutorial will guide you from a fresh installation to a fully operational AI workforce. Follow these steps to transform your WordPress site into an AI-powered command center.

---

## Phase 1: Infrastructure Setup (The Skeleton)

### Step 1: Secure Your Engine
**Action:** Navigate to **Nexus AI > System Config**.
1. Enter your **OpenAI**, **Claude**, or **Gemini** API keys.
2. Click **Save Infrastructure**.
3. Run the **Platform Diagnostics** (Run Full Scan) to verify.
**What to expect:**
- The "System Integrity" monitor will show 13/13 tables and a "READY" Encryption Engine.
- Your keys are now encrypted with AES-256-CTR, protected even if your database is compromised.

### Step 2: Brand Your Command Center
**Action:** Scroll down to **Agency White Label**.
1. Upload your logo and select your primary brand color.
2. Save changes.
**What to expect:**
- The dashboard UI accents will instantly change to your brand color.
- If you are an agency, the "Nexus AI" name will be replaced by your chosen brand in client portals.

---

## Phase 2: Knowledge Ingestion (The Brain)

### Step 3: Feed the Company Brain
**Action:** Go to **Nexus AI > Company Brain**.
1. Upload your Company SOPs (PDF), Product Catalog (CSV), and Brand Voice guidelines.
2. Enter your website URL in the **URL Scraper** and click **Index**.
**What to expect:**
- You will see a progress bar as the system chunks and indexes your data.
- Once complete, the "Memory Integrity" panel will update with your total indexed documents.
- **Result:** Every agent you hire later will now have access to this shared intelligence.

---

## Phase 3: Workforce Deployment (The Talent)

### Step 4: Hire Your Executive Team
**Action:** Go to **Nexus AI > Hire AI Agents**.
1. **CEO:** Hire a CEO agent with a "Visionary" communication style and high "KPI focus."
2. **CMO:** Hire a CMO agent powered by **Claude 3.5** for superior creative writing.
3. **Developer:** Hire a Developer agent with **Temperature 0.2** for precise, logic-heavy code.
**What to expect:**
- A "Deployment Successful" notification.
- Your agents will appear in the "Active Workforce" list, ready for orders.

---

## Phase 4: Collaboration & Execution (The Output)

### Step 5: Deploy the Public Widget & Client Portal
**Action:**
1. **Floating Widget:** Go to **Nexus AI > System Config > Frontend Chat Widget**. Toggle **Enable Public Widget**.
2. **Secure Portal:** Create a new WordPress Page (e.g., "Client HQ"). Paste the shortcode `[nexus_ai_portal]`.
3. Save and view the page.
**What to expect:**
- A floating glassmorphism chat bubble will appear on your public website.
- The "Client HQ" page will transform into a luxury, full-screen AI Command Center for your logged-in clients.

### Step 6: Start a Strategy Meeting
**Action:** Open the **Nexus Chat** (Overview or Sidebar).
1. Click **Start Meeting**.
2. Invite your CEO, CMO, and CFO agents.
3. Topic: "Analyze our Q3 performance and suggest a Q4 scaling plan."
**What to expect:**
- Real-time interaction as agents debate. The CMO suggests ads, the CFO warns about budget, and the CEO synthesizes the final plan.
- At the end, you receive "Meeting Minutes" in Markdown format.

### Step 7: Launch an Automation
**Action:** Go to **Nexus AI > Automations**.
1. Open the **Visual Builder**.
2. Drag your specialized agents onto the **Strategic Canvas**.
3. Define the tasks and click **Save Workflow**.
4. Click **RUN** on the saved automation.
**What to expect:**
- A **Workflow Execution Trace** modal will open.
- You can watch the real-time "Reasoning" and "Output" of each agent as they pass the state forward.
- **Final Result:** A high-quality, SEO-optimized draft post will appear in your WordPress **Posts > Drafts** folder, or other actions as defined.

## Phase 5: Subscriptions, Licensing, & Dynamic Enterprise Management

### Step 8: Configure Licensing & Plan Limits
**Action:** Navigate to **Nexus AI > License & Payments**.
1. **Dynamic Pricing:** Administrators can go to the **Pricing & Gateways** tab to dynamically modify pricing defaults for:
   - **Pro Plan Price:** Defaults to `$197.00/mo` (limit: 10 active agents).
   - **Agency Plan Price:** Defaults to `$497.00/mo` (limit: 100 active agents).
   - **Enterprise Plan Price:** Defaults to `$997.00/mo` (limit: unlimited agents & custom models).
2. **Key Generation:** Under the **License Key Registry** tab, select a plan tier (e.g., Enterprise) and click **Generate License Key**.
3. **Key Activation:** On the client or local side under **Client Activation**, paste the generated key and click **Activate Local License** to unlock the corresponding workspace limits.

### Step 9: Strategic Archive Management
**Action:** Navigate to **Nexus AI > Strategic Archive**.
1. To permanently delete old transcripts, expired meeting recordings, or redundant traces, locate the session row in the Archive.
2. Click **Delete**. Confirm the prompt to clean and prune messages from the database.
