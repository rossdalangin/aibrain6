================================================================================
NEXUS AI WORKFORCE - ENTERPRISE LICENSING & BILLING INTEGRATION GUIDE
================================================================================

This step-by-step master guide explains how to set up Pricing Plans, configure
Payment Gateways (Stripe vs. PayPal), deploy Shortcodes on your sales pages,
customize the post-checkout Thank You page messages, format Email Notifications,
and activate License Keys in your Nexus AI Workforce workspace.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SETTING UP PLANS & CHANGING THE PRICING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
By default, Nexus AI Workforce has three preset premium tiers (Pro, Agency, and
Enterprise). You can change their prices and metrics directly from the admin:

1. Navigate to your WordPress Admin dashboard.
2. Go to: Nexus AI > License & Payments (or admin.php?page=nexus-ai-licensing).
3. Click on the "Pricing & Gateways" tab.
4. Locate the pricing inputs:
   - "Pro Plan Monthly Price ($)" (Default is 197.00)
   - "Agency Plan Monthly Price ($)" (Default is 497.00)
   - "Enterprise Plan Monthly Price ($)" (Default is 997.00)
5. Update the prices to your preferred rates and select your preferred Base Currency
   (USD, EUR, or GBP) from the dropdown.
6. Click "Save Gateway & Pricing Configuration" to secure and apply changes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SETTING UP THE PAYMENT GATEWAYS (STRIPE vs. PAYPAY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can process secure recurring client subscriptions using either Stripe or PayPal:

1. Navigate to: Nexus AI > License & Payments > Pricing & Gateways tab.
2. Select your gateway from the "Select Primary Payment Gateway" dropdown:
   - Select "Stripe Subscription API" or "PayPal Subscriptions".
3. In the right-hand panel, configure your credentials:
   - For Stripe: Paste your Stripe Publishable Client ID (`pk_live_...`) and your Secret Key (`sk_live_...`).
   - For PayPal: Paste your PayPal Business Client ID and Secret Token.
4. Choose the Billing Mode:
   - Sandbox / Test Environment: (Recommended during checkout setup to test stubs).
   - Production / Live Environment: (Use this for live client checkouts).
5. Click "Save Gateway & Pricing Configuration" to bind the payment handler.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ADDING THE PRICING TABLE TO A SALES PAGE (SHORTCODE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To display a beautiful, responsive, and interactive pricing table grid on your
sales or marketing landing pages:

1. Create or edit a WordPress page (e.g., your "Pricing" or "Sales" page).
2. Insert the following pricing shortcode:
   [nexus_pricing_table]
3. Save or publish the page. This shortcode will automatically render three professional,
   premium-styled layout cards (Starter, Pro, and Agency) with your configured prices and
   plan limits. Clicking "Subscribe" on any plan triggers our simulated SaaS checkout loop.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CONFIGURING THE "THANK YOU" PAGE & PURCHASE COMPLETED MESSAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Immediately after a customer successfully subscribes and completes their payment,
they should be redirected to a beautiful "Thank You" page displaying their order
details and generated License Key:

1. Create a new WordPress page and slug-name it "thank-you" (URL: /thank-you).
2. Insert the following thank you shortcode:
   [nexus_thank_you]
3. Save and publish this page.
4. To customize the text shown to users:
   - Navigate to: Nexus AI > License & Payments > Checkout & Email Settings tab.
   - Edit the "Thank You Message Template" field.
   - Use these supported dynamic variables to personalize the output:
     * `{customer_name}`: Resolves to the customer's name.
     * `{plan_name}`: Resolves to the purchased plan level (e.g., PRO, AGENCY).
     * `{license_key}`: Resolves to the cryptographically unique license key generated for their subscription.
   - Click "Save Email & Thank You Templates".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CUSTOMIZING EMAIL NOTIFICATIONS (LICENSE KEY DISPATCH)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every successful subscription automatically dispatches an elegant, high-impact
email notification to the customer's inbox containing their licensing details:

1. Navigate to: Nexus AI > License & Payments > Checkout & Email Settings tab.
2. Go to the "Email Notification Template" panel.
3. Customize the "Email Subject" (e.g., "Your Nexus AI Workforce Premium License Key").
4. Customize the "Email Body Content". You can structure the email to explain how to
   use the key and support active links.
5. Use the supported placeholders: `{customer_name}`, `{plan_name}`, and `{license_key}`.
6. Click "Save Email & Thank You Templates" to bind the transaction notifier.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. HOW THE USER ACTIVATES THE LICENSE KEY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Once a customer receives their license key (either on the Thank You screen or via
their automated email inbox), they can activate it on their site:

1. Copy the license key, e.g., `NEXUS-PRO-ABCD-EFGH`.
2. Go to their local WordPress Admin dashboard.
3. Navigate to: Nexus AI > License & Payments.
4. Click on the "Client Activation" tab.
5. Paste the key in the "License Key" input field.
6. Click "Activate Local License".
7. Our secure activation protocol will make a remote verification call, sign the option,
   and unlock their workspace limits securely:
   - Starter Tier: Restricted to 1 active agent.
   - Pro Tier: Unlocks up to 10 active agents.
   - Agency Tier: Unlocks up to 100 active agents.
   - Enterprise Tier: Unlocks unlimited active agents and vector modules.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6B. ENTERPRISE PRICING MANAGEMENT & FULFILLMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Enterprise-level clients often require custom billing rates, dedicated support SLAs,
and specialized multi-agent parameters (such as Pinecone / Weaviate vector DB).
Here is how administrators manage and fulfill Enterprise subscriptions:

1. MANAGING ENTERPRISE PRICING:
   - Go to Nexus AI > License & Payments > Pricing & Gateways tab.
   - Edit the "Enterprise Plan Monthly Price ($)" input.
   - Click "Save Gateway & Pricing Configuration".
   - Both your settings dashboard and the Client Billing Page (Plans & Billing)
     will automatically synchronize and render this pricing rate dynamically.

2. GENERATING & FULFILLING ENTERPRISE LICENSES:
   - Go to the "License Key Registry" tab.
   - Choose "Enterprise Plan" from the Plan Tier dropdown.
   - Click "Generate License Key". The key will be cryptographically signed.
   - Provide the key to the Enterprise client.
   - When the client activates the key on their side, our protocol automatically:
     * Unlocks unlimited active agents in their workspace.
     * Activates the premium Local Ollama and Cloud vector search module.
     * Configures dedicated support communication hooks on their dashboard.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. AUDITING TRANSACTIONS & SIGNATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
As a site administrator, you can audit all transactions, license bindings, and Stripe
payments:
- Go to Nexus AI > License & Payments > "License Key Registry" tab to see all generated keys, plan tiers, and their active binding domains.
- Go to the "Stripe Billing Logs" tab to see Stripe charge IDs, date, customer email, amount paid, and charge status syncs.
