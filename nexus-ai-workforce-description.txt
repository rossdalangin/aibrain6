NEXUS AI WORKFORCE: THE ULTI-PREMIUM MULTI-AGENT AI OPERATING SYSTEM (AIOS)
================================================================================

Nexus AI Workforce is an enterprise-grade Multi-Agent Collaboration and Artificial
Intelligence Operating System (AIOS) built natively for WordPress. Rather than
interacting with static, siloed chatbots, Nexus AI Workforce allows businesses,
founders, and digital agencies to deploy an entire virtual executive suite of
highly specialized AI employees that share a centralized memory and autonomously
collaborate to deliver finished, high-impact business outcomes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CORE VALUE PROPOSITION & SYSTEM PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Stop Prompts, Start Outcomes:
  Generic AI tools require continuous manual context-switching, prompting, and
  human oversight. Nexus turns AI into a team. By assigning specific Strategic
  KPIs, reasoning styles, and action guardrails to specialized personas (e.g. CEO,
  CMO, Developer), businesses can delegate complete tasks and watch their virtual
  team debate, reach consensus, and execute actions autonomously.

* Absolute Data Sovereignty (Self-Hosted Hybrid Model):
  In an era of cloud-privacy concerns, Nexus AI Workforce stores all corporate
  documents, operational SOPs, and chat logs directly in your local WordPress
  database. Your intellectual property never trains public models.

* Ultra-Premium Design Mastery:
  A premium, high-impact dashboard interface engineered with modern flat layouts,
  highly polished light-theme contrast, visual grids, and zero layout friction.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. KEY SYSTEM MODULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* 🏢 HIRE AI AGENTS (THE PERSONA WIZARD)
  Deploy expert talent instantly using battle-tested templates or custom profiles.
  Configure every detail of an agent's DNA: Professional Position, Core Mission,
  Skills, Communication Tone, Voice, LLM Provider (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, Gemini 1.5),
  Thinking Process, and Negative Constraints (Guardrails) to prevent hallucinations.

* 🧠 COMPANY BRAIN (MULTI-TIERED RAG ENGINE)
  Ingest proprietary knowledge recursively from PDFs, DOCX, TXT, or live website
  scraping. Nexus organizes intelligence into Global, Department, and Agent-level
  tiers. Agents securely query this "shared IQ" to ground their answers with
  factual, company-specific contexts.

* 🤝 COLLABORATION HUB (VIRTUAL MEETING ROOMS)
  Invite multiple AI specialists into strategy meetings on dynamic agendas. Watch
  in real-time as they critique each other, debate, build alignment, and execute
  autonomous consensus loops until they reach a formal decision keyword.

* ⚙️ MULTI-AGENT AUTOMATIONS (VISUAL BUILDER CANVAS)
  Orchestrate advanced operational flows on a highly responsive, high-contrast,
  light-themed visual drag-and-drop canvas. Chain agents linearly (e.g., SEO Researcher
  -> Conversion Copywriter -> WP Developer) so outputs persist forward as direct
  context for the next step, producing fully drafted WordPress posts automatically.

* 📚 LEARNING CENTER (ELITE PROMPTING PLAYBOOK)
  An interactive, premium learning dashboard to help users master workspace limits.
  Includes a fully copyable prompting handbook and SOP templates for high-velocity roles.

* 🗄️ STRATEGIC ARCHIVE (WITH SECURE SESSION DELETION)
  A centralized database of past sessions, workflow traces, and transcripts. Contains
  a single-click session deletion tool that securely purges both parent conversations
  and nested messages from the database to maintain clean database records.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SaaS REVENUE, DYNAMIC BILLING & CRYPTOGRAPHIC LICENSING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Nexus AI Workforce is paired with a Licensing & Payment SaaS Bridge, allowing creators
to sell AI-as-a-Service subscriptions directly through secure payment loops (Stripe/PayPal):

* Flexible, Standardized Tiers and Pricing:
  - STARTER PLAN ($0/mo): Perfect for testing. Restricts to 1 active agent.
  - PROFESSIONAL PLAN ($197/mo): Up to 10 active agents and advanced RAG uploads.
  - AGENCY PLAN ($497/mo): Up to 100 active agents, white-labeling, and client portals.
  - ENTERPRISE PLAN ($997/mo): Unlimited agents, Local Ollama hosting, Pinecone DB.

* Dynamic Price Management:
  Administrators can dynamically configure and modify the Pro, Agency, and Enterprise
  monthly rates directly from the admin dashboard (Pricing & Gateways settings page).
  Changes instantly synchronize across the licensing backend and the Client Billing pages.

* Secure Cryptographic Fulfillment:
  When a user purchases a plan, the master server generates a cryptographically-signed
  license key via a secure HMAC-SHA256 signature using a shared master secret salt.
  Upon client activation, the local workspace validates the signature to unlock agent limits.
  Any database tampering or signature mismatch automatically downgrades the site back
  to the Starter tier, ensuring bulletproof license lock-shielding.
