================================================================================
NEXUS AI WORKFORCE: VISUAL WORKFLOW CANVAS PIPELINE TASK EXAMPLES
================================================================================

This document outlines distinct, real-world examples of multi-agent task pipelines
to construct inside the Visual Workflow Canvas. Use these templates to easily
understand how agents collaborate, how context passes forward, and what output
results to expect in your WordPress workspace.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXAMPLE 1: THE CONTENT AUTOMATION MACHINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Pipeline Purpose:
  Research keywords, draft a premium SEO-optimized article based on the checklist,
  and automatically publish it as a WordPress Draft post.

* Step 1: Assigned Agent: Sarah (SEO Keyword Analyst)
  - Task Input:
    "Analyze current industry keywords for 'enterprise artificial intelligence' and
    'AI multi-agent workforce' and select 3 high-impact keywords. Output only the
    keyword list and their search intent."
  - Expected Output:
    A bulleted list of 3 selected keywords (e.g. 'autonomous AI teams') and search intents.

* Step 2: Assigned Agent: Elena (Growth Architect / Copywriter)
  - Task Input:
    "Write a high-converting, premium introduction and structure outline for a
    blog post based on the 3 selected keywords from Step 1. Incorporate the SEO SOP
    guidelines: Keep sentences under 20 words and paragraph lengths short (3-4 sentences max)."
  - Expected Input Received:
    The keyword list output from Step 1 is automatically passed in as background context.
  - Expected Output:
    A beautifully structured introductory copy and outline including H2 headings.

* Step 3: Assigned Agent: Alexander (Chief Strategy Officer / Editor)
  - Task Input:
    "Review the outline from Step 2. Expand it into a full 800-word draft article.
    Ensure our brand values of 'Data Sovereignty' and 'ROI Maximization' are explicitly
    highlighted. Format in clean Markdown."
  - Expected Input Received:
    The introductory copy and outline from Step 2 is automatically passed in as context.
  - Expected Output:
    A full, publication-ready, markdown-formatted draft blog post.
  - Terminal WordPress State:
    Upon completion, a brand-new post containing this exact text is created inside your
    WordPress Admin under Posts > All Posts (Status: Draft), ready for your final review.

EXAMPLE 2: THE WOOCOMMERCE PRODUCT LAUNCH PIPELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Pipeline Purpose:
  Analyze product positioning and competitive strategies, draft premium product descriptions,
  and automatically insert the new product into WooCommerce with pricing metadata.

* Step 1: Assigned Agent: Alexander (Chief Strategy Officer)
  - Task Input:
    "Review our target segment for our premium 'Nexus AI Workforce Operating System'.
    Identify 3 major value pillars and positioning strategy against solo chatbot SaaS apps."
  - Expected Output:
    A strategic positioning brief highlighting Multi-Agent Orchestration and White-Label options.

* Step 2: Assigned Agent: Elena (Growth Architect)
  - Task Input:
    "Persuasively write a benefit-driven product description for WooCommerce based on
    the positioning strategy in Step 1. Ensure the description contains high-converting
    calls to action. Structure as short paragraphs with bullet points of key features."
  - Expected Output:
    A compelling WooCommerce product description body in HTML/Markdown.

* Step 3: Assigned Agent: Marcus (Systems Architect / WooCommerce Connector)
  - Task Input:
    "Create a WooCommerce product using the description from Step 2. Set the regular
    price as $497.00/mo (matching our Agency Plan) and map it to the Category 'Software Services'."
  - Expected Output:
    Triggering of the native WooCommerce Create Product Action API.
  - Terminal WordPress State:
    A new, live product titled 'Nexus AI Workforce OS' is created in WooCommerce > Products,
    fully populated with the description, price ($497.00), and categorization.

EXAMPLE 3: THE CUSTOMER SUPPORT FAQ AUTOMATOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Pipeline Purpose:
  Parse common support inquiries, query the Company Brain for the matching policy,
  and generate an elegant email reply template for the support team.

* Step 1: Assigned Agent: Support Parser (Simple Agent)
  - Task Input:
    "Read this incoming ticket from customer_inquiry@company.com: 'Hi, I bought the
    Pro Plan 8 days ago and wanted to know if I cancel, will I lose my data and can
    I get a refund?' Extract the core question."
  - Expected Output:
    A summary of queries: Refund eligibility (Day 8 purchase) and cancellation data impact.

* Step 2: Assigned Agent: Sarah (Knowledge Base Liaison / Support Manager)
  - Task Input:
    "Query our Refund & Cancellation Policy brain to find the exact rules regarding:
    1) 14-day Risk-Free Guarantee refund rules.
    2) What happens to client files and databases when a subscription is cancelled."
  - Expected Output:
    Retrieves policy fragments (Day 8 is within 14-day window = 100% refund; cancellation
    safe-downgrades to Free tier on expiration date without breaking database records).

* Step 3: Assigned Agent: Elena (Growth Architect)
  - Task Input:
    "Compose a highly professional, empathetic, and reassuring email response to
    customer_inquiry@company.com based on the policy details retrieved in Step 2.
    Provide clear step-by-step instructions on how to cancel and assure them their
    files are completely safe."
  - Expected Output:
    A complete email template draft with dynamic parameters, ready to copy and send.
